How the rankings work

52-week rolling

Points earned at a tournament count for 52 weeks, then drop off. Defend them or lose them.

Best 19 (ATP) / 18 (WTA)

The four Slams, the Masters/1000s and a limited number of best other results count. The Tour Finals are a bonus on top.

Points per title

Grand Slam 2000 · Finals up to 1500 · Masters 1000 · 500 · 250. A Slam runner-up earns 1300.

Race vs. ranking

The 'Race' counts only this calendar year and decides who qualifies for the season-ending Finals.
