What goes in the bag

Everything a player carries, from the two rackets you cannot play without to the training tools that make the animations on this site useful.

Each item links to an Amazon search and to the brands' official shops. Tennis Lab has no affiliate deals; links are for convenience and open in a new tab.

Shoes by surface

Hard court

Durable outsole (look for a 6-month outsole guarantee), lateral support, cushioning. The all-rounder most people need.

Clay

Full herringbone tread that releases clay and lets you slide. Tighter upper to keep clay out.

Grass

Small rubber nubs ('pimples') for traction without damaging the turf. Required at most grass clubs.

Carpet / indoor

Smooth, non-marking outsole with grip but no deep tread.
